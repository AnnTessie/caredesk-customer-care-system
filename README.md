# CareDesk – Customer Care & Lead Management System

A centralized customer care and lead management system designed to help businesses manage customer communication, support requests and follow-ups from one dashboard.

## Overview

CareDesk brings customer service operations into one workspace, helping teams organize calls, WhatsApp conversations, customer records, support tickets, leads and follow-ups.

## Key Features

- Call Management
- WhatsApp Conversation Management
- Customer Management
- Support Tickets
- Lead Management
- Follow-Up Tracking
- Owner Approval Queue
- Staff Management
- Reports & Analytics
- Quick Replies
- FAQ & Automation
- Customer Search
- Dashboard & Performance Overview

## Project Goal

The goal was to create a simple system that helps businesses reduce missed customer inquiries, organize leads and improve follow-up workflows.
